# SmartFabrication — Hindi + English Translation & Terminology Guide

## 1. Principles of Rural & Small-Town Hindi Localization

1. **Colloquial & Familiar**: Use the Hindi language spoken in Indian markets, construction sites, and households.
2. **Keep Established English Words**: Terms like **Gate, Grill, Railing, Steel, WhatsApp, Feet, Inches, Design, Photo, Order** are universally understood across Indian villages. Translating them into obscure words (e.g., using "द्वार" instead of "गेट" or "पाद" instead of "फीट") causes confusion and must be avoided.
3. **Warm & Trustworthy**: Focus on strength ("मजबूत"), beauty ("सुंदर"), long life ("सालों-साल चले"), and accurate site measurement ("घर पर आकर सही नाप लेना").
4. **No Unsupported Claims**: Avoid exaggerated marketing jargon ("Aerospace grade", "Zero-tolerance CAD precision", "Guaranteed 48-hour dispatch").

## 3. Order Milestones Mapping

| Existing Database Status | Rural Hindi Display Status | Customer Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `CONFIRMED` | **1. ऑर्डर मिल गया** | Your order request has been accepted by the workshop. |
| `ADVANCE_RECEIVED` | **1. ऑर्डर मिल गया (एडवांस प्राप्त)** | Advance payment recorded; project queued. |
| `MEASUREMENT_CONFIRMED` | **2. नाप की जाँच पूरी** | Technical staff verified exact on-site dimensions. |
| `PRODUCTION_PENDING` | **2. नाप की जाँच पूरी (तैयारी जारी)** | Material allocated; awaiting workshop slot. |
| `PRODUCTION_STARTED` | **3. काम शुरू हो गया** | Cutting, welding, and framing in progress. |
